# Google — AI Plus

Source check: **2026-09-20**.

## Facts

* US price: **$4.99/month**; **400 GB storage**.
* Gemini usage advertised at **2× Free**, versus **4× Free** for Pro. [Subscriptions](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/).

## Representative usage

Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview comparator: **$4.45/block**, including assumed cache storage. [Calculation](proPlan.md); [API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing#gemini-3.1-pro-preview).

## Monthly estimate — conditional scaling

Assume actual monthly throughput is **2 / 4 = 0.5×** the hypothetical Pro baseline:

**(26–30) × 0.5 = 13–15 blocks/month.**

**13–15 × $4.45 = $57.85–$66.75/month**, central **$62.30**.

* API-equivalent multiple: **11.59–13.38×**.
* Central INR illustration: **₹5,607**, against converted subscription **₹449**.
* Break-even: **$4.99 / $4.45 = 1.12 blocks/month**.

## Key findings

The advertised access ratio motivates this scenario; it does not prove a monthly token allowance or identical model availability. At **one block/month**, replacement cost is only **$4.45**, below the subscription price.

Storage and media benefits are excluded. The larger multiple than Pro follows from the assumed half-usage allowance at roughly one-quarter the price. [Methodology](../methodology.md).
